Worth your time, in my opinion.

Privacy

  • GrapheneOSPhone operating system

  • ProtonEmail, storage, VPN

How things work

  • CodeHow computers work, built up from nothing. Critical for understanding today’s technology.Charles Petzold
  • Chip WarChris Miller
  • SandSomething you never think about until you realize how important it is.Michael Welland
  • The Singularity Is NearNo explanation needed at this time.Ray Kurzweil

Math and physics

  • How to Prove ItThe best book I’ve found on learning to write real proofs.Daniel Velleman
  • Excursions in Number TheoryJust a fun math book.C. Stanley Ogilvy and John T. Anderson
  • The Elegant UniverseIf this doesn’t get you interested in physics, I don’t know what will.Brian Greene

Minds

  • How the Mind WorksI read it in high school. It really helps frame thinking.Steven Pinker
  • Why Zebras Don’t Get UlcersAbout far more than ulcers and stress: free will, and how society should be structured.Robert Sapolsky
  • DeterminedSapolsky saying outright what he was really saying in Zebras.Robert Sapolsky
  • BuzzedFrom an undergrad class on drugs and behavior. Oddly, one of the best introductions to neuroscience.Cynthia Kuhn, Scott Swartzwelder and Wilkie Wilson
  • How to Change Your MindMichael Pollan
