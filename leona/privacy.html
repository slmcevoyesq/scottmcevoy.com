Privacy notice

Last updated October 10, 2026.

Who we are

Leona is made by Scott McEvoy. Contact: info@scottmcevoy.com.

This website (scottmcevoy.com/leona)

  • The site is a plain page. It sets no cookies and loads no analytics, advertising, tracking scripts or fonts from other companies.
  • The site is hosted by GitHub (GitHub Pages). As with any web host, your browser’s request reaches GitHub’s servers. See GitHub’s own privacy statement for what it records.
  • Downloading Leona does not require an account or any personal details.

Requesting a test license

  • You request a license by email to info@scottmcevoy.com. We receive your email address and whatever you write.
  • We use your address only to send your license and news about the test.
  • Your email is handled by our email provider (Proton). See Proton’s own privacy policy for how it handles mail.

Your license code

  • A license code holds the kind of license, the date it was issued, the date it ends, and a random identifier. It holds no name and no email address.
  • Leona checks the code on your Mac. It does not contact us, or anyone, to check it.

The Leona app

  • No analytics. Leona connects only to the AI providers, feeds and websites you choose, and to scottmcevoy.com for the update check you can turn off.
  • Once a day, and when you press Check for updates, Leona asks scottmcevoy.com whether a newer build exists. The request carries no identifier and nothing about your chats or Library; like any visit to a website, it shows your internet address to the site's host. You can turn the daily check off in Settings.
  • A chat you lock with a password is sealed with its own key. Leona does not store your password, and without the password or the recovery code nobody can open the chat, including Leona. If you lose both, the chat cannot be recovered.
  • In a Private conversation, your messages go only to a model running on this Mac.
  • Your knowledge base is plain text files in a folder you choose. They stay readable without this app.
  • Conversations are stored encrypted on this Mac.
  • Nothing is added to your knowledge base until you approve it.
  • Before sending to a cloud model, Leona looks for common identifiers such as emails and card numbers, the terms you list, and, with the name finder on, names of people, places and organizations, and offers to hide them. It misses things and sometimes flags harmless words, so keep anything truly sensitive on a local model with Private on.
  • Your API keys are stored in the macOS Keychain.
  • When you choose a cloud AI provider (Anthropic, OpenAI or OpenRouter) with your own key, what you send goes to that provider under your own agreement with them. See their privacy policies.
  • When you add an RSS feed or open a web page in Leona’s browser, that website receives the request, as with any browser.

Children

Leona is not directed at children under 13.
